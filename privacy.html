Introduction

DEVA Photography (“we”, “us”, “our”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we receive when you use this website or contact us, why we receive it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

Booking form

  • Full name
  • Phone number
  • Event type (for example wedding, fashion, portrait or baby shoot)

Contact form

  • Name
  • Phone number
  • Email address (optional)
  • Your message

How the forms work

Our forms do not store your details on this website. When you press submit, your details are checked in your browser and placed into a WhatsApp message on your own device. Nothing is sent to us until you choose to press Send in WhatsApp.

Other contact

If you email, call or message us directly, we receive the information you choose to include, such as your name, contact details and event information.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your enquiry and share availability and package details.
  • To plan, confirm and deliver the photography services you book.
  • To send you your photographs and related updates.
  • To keep basic records of bookings and payments where required by law.

We do not sell your personal information.
